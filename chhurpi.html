The Ancient Process

Our cheese is made from the milk of free-roaming Yaks and Cows grazing on alpine pastures in Nepal.

  • Step 1: Fresh milk is boiled and churned to separate the cream.
  • Step 2: The remaining buttermilk is boiled again until solid mass forms.
  • Step 3: The solids are vigorously pressed in thin jute cloth to drain all water.
  • Step 4: Cut into precise blocks and hung over traditional wood fires.
  • Step 5: Slow-smoked for 35 to 40 days to achieve maximum hardness.

Sourced Locally: We partner directly with local farmers in Khandbari and the Koshi Province, ensuring fair wages and sustaining ancient farming practices.
